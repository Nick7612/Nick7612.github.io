My Story

I'm a car guy first. I started reviewing cars as ThatCarGuyNick on YouTube and Instagram, then got serious about the hands-on side: working in an autobody shop, shooting car photography, and installing car audio systems for real cars.

At home I built my own Bluetooth speaker from scratch — wiring, enclosure, tuning — and at school I'm a Mechanical Engineering Technology major at NJIT, where I mix shop skills with engineering math and design.

I also code. I've built weather apps and news apps using live APIs and data, because I like projects where software meets the real world.
